// about

I'm a Computer Science and Mathematics student at LSU with Digital Media AVATAR Arts Minor. And currently working at the LSU-Center for Computation & Technology as a Student Worker until graduation. The responsibilities include, but are not limited to :

  • Provide service and technical support for Digital Twin, 3D modeling, and digital media activities.
  • Assist faculty, staff, students, and COE partners with digital tools, equipment, and project needs.
  • Assist with equipment setup, basic troubleshooting, and preparation of digital media resources.

Software and Game Development are my two focuses. I studied Game Design in Berlin specifically for the artistic side of it, and that's where coding and art stopped feeling like separate things to me. I like building the systems as much as I like making them feel like something.

Outside of school I shoot photography, edit montages in Premiere Pro, and draw a bit too. I love traveling, and I've spent real stretches of time living and studying across Berlin, Munich, Paris, and Baton Rouge — which mostly means I'm equally comfortable debugging in English, ordering in French, and apologizing for my German.
